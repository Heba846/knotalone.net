# Backlinks & distribution — knotalone.net

Researched November 2026 via live search. Page contents could not be opened from the build
sandbox, so **check each live page before you act on it** — rules change, bans don't un-ring.

Two constraints shape everything here:

1. **You are not a licensed clinician.** That rules out the big provider directories
   (Psychology Today, ChoosingTherapy, Mentalyc). Your lane is *educator / printable-maker /
   author*, and that is a real, pitchable identity. Never imply clinical authority — editors
   check, and it breaks your own brand rule.
2. **Keep the free printables ungated.** This is not a marketing opinion. OER Commons requires
   resources be "openly accessible without creating an account", and university counselling
   centres will not link a page that demands an email. Gate the *bundles*, give away the *singles*.
   The seven PDFs on `/free/` are currently ungated — keep them that way.

## 1. Directories — only four are worth your time

Mass directory submission is a dead channel. Ignore every "290+ free directory sites" list and
every 1-click submission tool: they produce footprint, not traffic.

| Target | URL | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **OER Commons** | [oercommons.org](https://oercommons.org) ([guidelines](https://help.oercommons.org/support/solutions/articles/42000046850-submission-guidelines)) | **Best single listing on this page.** Free account → "Add OER" → "Add Link". Real .org authority, educator audience, free worksheets are a native fit. Requires the PDF be openly accessible with no login. |
| **MERLOT — Psychology** | [merlot.org/merlot/Psychology.htm](https://merlot.org/merlot/Psychology.htm) | Submit **one mind game**, not the site. An academic library pointing at a free interactive psychology demo is exactly what MERLOT collects. |
| **Curlie** | [curlie.org/desc/Health/Mental_Health](https://curlie.org/desc/Health/Mental_Health) ([how to submit](https://curlie.org/docs/en/help/submit_html)) | 20 minutes, one time, then forget it. Human-edited, still alive. Pick the most specific category and **submit once** — resubmitting hurts. |
| **MHC Resource Directory** | [mhconn.org](https://www.mhconn.org) | Free, and explicitly accepts *educational resources*, not only providers. Reads US-regional, so it may decline — 10 minutes to find out. |

**Also claim, for the books:** [Goodreads Author Program](https://www.goodreads.com/author/program)
(free, any country — mandatory once a book is listed) and
[BookBub Author Profile](https://www.bookbub.com/partners/author-profile) (free; ignore the paid
Featured Deals). For a polished mind game: [itch.io](https://itch.io) (free HTML5 hosting + a real
backlink; do not use unrelated tags) and one [Product Hunt](https://www.producthunt.com) launch.

**Do not pay for:** dualdiagnosis.org.uk's £1 listing, the $295 All About Psychology Substack
sponsorship, Kirkus Indie, or The Children's Book Review's paid "Dedicated Reviews". Paid links
are discounted by Google and they are a brand risk for a site whose rule is "no invented statistics".

## 3. Communities — where a link gets you banned

### Do not post links here

| Community | The rule |
|---|---|
| **r/ADHD** | Rule 8, no self-promotion — an older wiki version says **"not even 'free' items"** and that posters will be banned. |
| **r/adhdwomen** | Mods posted in Oct 2025 about "an uptick in accounts trying to market tools". Actively hostile to link drops. |
| **r/therapy** | "It is explicitly against our rules to post promotions and advertisements." |
| **r/MentalHealthUK** | No self-promotion; naming your own venture counts. |
| **r/attachment_theory** | Mods have declined outside promotion. (Evidence from 2022 — posture is clear.) |
| **r/DecidingToBeBetter** | Breaking self-promo rules = removal and likely ban. |
| **Teacher / school-counsellor Facebook groups** | Group rules vary; read the pinned post. Where a "Marketing Monday" thread exists, it is mostly therapists marketing to therapists. |

### Usable, with conditions

| Community | The rule | How to use it |
|---|---|---|
| **r/CPTSD** | Self-promo allowed **in the weekly Vents & Victories / check-in thread only** — the clearest written permission found anywhere. | Post there, never top-level. |
| **r/getdisciplined** | No link posts; **the link must be in the body** of a self-post. | Write real substance, one link as a source. This is the healthiest pattern generally. |
| **r/selfpublish** | Weekly self-promo thread. | For the novels and picture books only. |
| **r/adhd_college** | Self-promotion fine unless it is an advertisement. | Small, but legitimate for a focus/study worksheet. Does **not** transfer to r/ADHD. |

**Discords:** no server's policy could be confirmed. Universal move: **DM a moderator, offer the
worksheet, let them post it.** Two minutes, converts better than a link drop, cannot get you banned.

**Quora:** substantive answers tolerate a link. But referrals are reportedly under 1% of Quora's
traffic — treat it as a *Google-ranking and credibility* play, not a traffic channel. A handful of
excellent answers on Overthinking, Rumination, Attachment, Breakups and ADHD. Not a daily habit.

## 5. Pinterest — the biggest channel here, by far

Bigger than every backlink on this page combined. Tactics below are vendor-sourced and some
contradict each other; treat them as hypotheses. The policy citations are first-party and binding.

**What reportedly works:**
- Keywords in **seven places**: profile name, bio, board names, board descriptions, pin titles, pin descriptions, image filenames/alt text.
- The **pin title carries the most weight** — under ~100 characters.
- **Do not keyword-stuff.** Viral pins mostly used five or fewer keywords in the description.
- **Front-load:** only ~50–60 characters show in feed, so the hook and the word "free" go first.
- **Fresh pins, not repins.** Repinning the same image across boards is now reportedly spam-flagged. Make several *different images* per worksheet instead.
- Free keyword research: Pinterest's own **search autocomplete**, Trends and Analytics.

**The policy line that matters for this niche.** Pinterest prohibits content that displays or
rationalises self-harm, and its [help article](https://help.pinterest.com/en/articles/suicide-and-self-harm)
names **"negative self-talk"** among prohibited categories. Educational content is allowed but
distribution may be limited. Enforcement is automated and aggressive.

**Translation:** your "educational, never diagnostic, no shaming" rule *is* your Pinterest
compliance strategy. Pin text names a **skill or action** ("a 5-minute worksheet to slow a spiral"),
never a symptom list, a diagnosis, or a self-critical quote. Avoid aesthetic quote pins entirely —
the exact format that performs in this niche is the one explicitly prohibited.

**Printable mechanics:** pin to the `/free/<slug>/` page, never to the PDF (a PDF link gives no
analytics, no second click, no internal links). Show the actual printable in the image — for a
printable, the mockup *is* the ad. Reuse the same vertical asset on Instagram and as a YouTube
Short.

## 6. The five highest-leverage actions, ranked

1. **Pitch the three Mind Remake Project roundups + Mental Health @ Home.** ~2 hours, highest hit-rate on this page. Their own pages admit many listed links are dead — you are the fix.
2. **Rebuild the Pinterest workflow** around fresh pins, seven-field keywords, and skill-framed copy. ~3 hours to set up, then weekly. The only channel that compounds without anyone's permission.
3. **Submit the worksheets to OER Commons and one mind game to MERLOT.** ~2 hours, one time, durable. Requires ungated PDFs.
4. **Land two guest posts: ADDitude + one from CollabStack.** ~6–8 hours each. ADDitude gives you a credential to reuse in every later pitch.
5. **Pitch six .edu counselling pages, starting with NDSU's rumination section.** ~3 hours, low hit rate, very high value per hit.

**Below the line:** any directory beyond the four above; Reddit link-dropping outside the three
permitted threads; daily Quora answering; any paid review or paid guest placement.

## The outreach email that fits every target in section 2

> **Subject:** a free rumination worksheet for your self-help library
>
> Hi [name] — I run knotalone.net, a free psychology-education site (explainers plus printable
> worksheets, all non-clinical and non-diagnostic).
>
> I noticed [their page] has a section on [rumination / free worksheets / ADHD printables] but no
> printable for it. I've made a one-page [worksheet name]: [URL]. It's free, no email signup, no
> paywall, and it's fine to link to or hand out directly.
>
> No obligation at all — just thought it might fill a gap. Happy to send a print-ready PDF if
> that's easier.
>
> [name], knotalone.net

Three things make this work, all non-negotiable: **it names their specific page**, **the resource
is genuinely ungated**, and **it asks for nothing.** Every version that adds "would you consider
linking to us?" converts worse.
