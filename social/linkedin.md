## adhd-time-blindness

```
Something that comes up constantly in groups: adhd time blindness — why "five minutes" is never five minutes.
Time blindness is difficulty sensing how much time has passed and how much remains.
What I keep seeing: the explanation itself does most of the work. People stop defending a behaviour once it stops looking like a character flaw and starts looking like a mechanism.
The version that travels well into a session: Set a visible timer for your next task — a kitchen timer, a phone timer face-up, anything with a shrinking shape. Not to hurry you. Just to find out what thirty minutes actually feels like, once, with your eyes instead of your gut.

## why-a-breakup-feels-like-withdrawal

```
Something that comes up constantly in groups: why a breakup feels like withdrawal, not just sadness.
